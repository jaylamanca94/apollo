## Incomplete flows and missing states

- All flows still need complete browser-backed keyboard-only, zoom/reflow, and screen-reader evidence. The Dashboard has accepted Vercel-runtime desktop plus controlled 390px and 320px partial states, an Enter-refresh check, and a pointer path from an unavailable source card to detailed recovery. Launches and Weather have accepted current 390px loaded, source-action, and controlled unavailable-recovery evidence; ISS/Crew has equivalent local-function-preview loaded evidence plus controlled unavailable recovery. Production/Vercel-platform, keyboard, zoom, reader, and hardware evidence for all three remains open. Asteroids has accepted 390px unavailable-recovery coverage with actions clear of the fixed dock, plus retry-focus evidence, but no browser-controller key activation; its loaded mobile state remains open.
- Asteroids and Gallery have accepted desktop loaded-state evidence from the local Vercel runtime using NASA's public exploration key. Gallery also has controlled 390px unavailable-recovery evidence; repeatable credential-backed, loaded mobile, alternate-media, and deployed-production evidence remains open. Local retry behaviour does not replace a resilience policy.
- The mobile Watch flow now has accepted pointer coverage for all three destinations and menu dismissal. Keyboard activation, hardware touch targets, zoom/reflow, and reader evidence remain open.
- The Dashboard intentionally has no retained-data state. Whether to add clearly aged last-known-good data is a founder decision, not an implementation gap to guess at.
- Sky Anomalies remains intentionally limited: it has no location-aware visibility, fireball, aircraft, planetary, satellite, or reported-sighting matching. Its live preview showed honest partial context and a source-aware submitted result at 390px and 320px; submission now moves focus to the result heading below the sticky header. The current controller focused controls but did not activate Enter or Space; real-browser keyboard-only submission, touch hardware, zoom, and reader evidence are still missing.

## Update rule

Before implementation, record flows added or changed, major states introduced, and required design and QA coverage. Keep each row as a distinct user goal from a meaningful entry point to a successful outcome; do not collapse flows merely to make the registry shorter.
