## Incomplete flows and missing states

- All flows still need complete browser-backed keyboard-only, zoom/reflow, and screen-reader evidence. The Dashboard has accepted Vercel-runtime desktop plus controlled 390px and 320px partial states, an Enter-refresh check, and a pointer path from an unavailable source card to detailed recovery. Launches, Weather, Asteroids, Gallery, and ISS/Crew now have controlled 320px unavailable-recovery coverage; Launches and Weather also have 390px loaded/source/recovery evidence. ISS/Crew’s upper recovery action now has accepted Space-key safe-scroll evidence, but touch/hardware confirmation remains material. Production/Vercel-platform, keyboard, zoom, reader, and hardware evidence remains open. Asteroids and Gallery still need loaded mobile evidence.
- Asteroids and Gallery have accepted desktop loaded-state evidence from the local Vercel runtime using NASA's public exploration key. Gallery also has controlled 390px unavailable-recovery evidence; repeatable credential-backed, loaded mobile, alternate-media, and deployed-production evidence remains open. Local retry behaviour does not replace a resilience policy.
- The mobile Watch flow now has accepted controlled keyboard coverage for Enter/Space opening, focus handoff, all three destinations, and menu dismissal at 390px. Hardware keyboard/touch targets, zoom/reflow, contrast, and reader evidence remain open.
- The Dashboard intentionally has no retained-data state. Whether to add clearly aged last-known-good data is a founder decision, not an implementation gap to guess at.
- Sky Anomalies remains intentionally limited: it has no location-aware visibility, fireball, aircraft, planetary, satellite, or reported-sighting matching. Its local preview has accepted keyboard selection and submission at 390px, with focus on the result heading below the sticky header; touch hardware, zoom, production, and reader evidence remain missing.

## Update rule

Before implementation, record flows added or changed, major states introduced, and required design and QA coverage. Keep each row as a distinct user goal from a meaningful entry point to a successful outcome; do not collapse flows merely to make the registry shorter.
