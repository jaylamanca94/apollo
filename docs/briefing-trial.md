# Apollo briefing comprehension and return trial

Prepared 2026-09-24 for 1.2.0. **Not run; no participant evidence exists.**

Hypothesis: curious non-specialists can understand the available picture, identify uncertainty and choose one worthwhile detail within two minutes, with less effort than their usual space-information source. This tests usefulness and repeat interest, not willingness to pay.

## Setup

Recruit five consenting occasional space followers through founder-approved outreach. No messages have been sent. Use an owner-accessible deployed revision after hosted acceptance, or a supervised local preview explicitly identified as local. Do not disable deployment protection for the trial. Record revision, viewport, date, source state and which alternative each participant normally uses. Use participant codes P1–P5; do not collect names, accounts, location history or recordings by default.

Prepare a source-backed loaded/partial state and an explicitly labelled all-source-failure fixture. Synthetic states test comprehension only. Capture the source timestamps at the start; do not imply dates remain current between sessions. Alternate Apollo-first versus usual-alternative-first order to reduce coaching effects.

## Uncoached tasks

1. Starting at the Dashboard, explain what you would follow and why. Open the relevant detail and its original source. Stop timing at the source action or two minutes.
2. Explain when the highlighted information applies and what “Last checked” means. Record target versus observation versus publication interpretation, including any confusion.
3. In the partial/failure state, explain what is known and what cannot be concluded. Record any inference that missing data means quiet activity or no hazards.
4. Try the equivalent check-in using your usual alternative. Record effort, completion time, what Apollo omitted and which you prefer, with the participant's own words.
5. Ask whether they would independently choose to revisit Apollo and for what occasion. Offer one optional check-in a week later through separately authorised contact; do not schedule or send it automatically.

Directional criteria: at least four of five distinguish missing from quiet and find a relevant action within two minutes; at least three independently choose to return. Record actual return separately from stated intent. These thresholds are an exploratory decision aid, not statistical validation. If source timestamps or coverage confuse readers, fix that specific friction. If the workflow adds no value over alternatives, reconsider the briefing proposition before accounts, paid alerts or new feeds.

Prerequisites: founder-approved recruitment/contact and accessible review environment; participant availability is external. Recheck when either becomes available. Prepared materials and implemented behaviour do not close this experiment.
